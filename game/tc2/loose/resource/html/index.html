Scout

Real Name Jeremy Willis
Origin Boston, Massachusetts, USA

Fast and agile with a focus on rapid strikes and hit-and-run.

The Scout is the fastest class in the game, excelling at quick skirmishes and hit-and-run attacks. His nimble frame and superior mobility make him ideal for capturing objectives and harassing enemies from unexpected angles. Scout thrives in close-quarters combat with his Scattergun and melee weapons, but must rely on speed and positioning to avoid taking heavy damage.

Biography

The youngest of eight boys from the south side of Boston, the Scout learned early how to problem solve with his fists. With seven older brothers on his side, fights tended to end before the runt of the litter could maneuver into punching distance, so the Scout trained himself to run. He ran everywhere, all the time, until he could beat his pack of mad dog siblings to the fray.

Soldier

Real Name Jane Doe
Origin Midwest, USA

Balanced offense class with rocket-based area damage and mobility.

The Soldier is a versatile offensive powerhouse, equipped with a Rocket Launcher for dealing significant splash damage. His mobility comes from Rocket Jumping, which allows a trained player to reach unexpected positions and control the fight. Soldier excels at mid-range combat and can deal consistent damage to both groups of enemies and single targets with good aim.

Biography

Though he wanted desperately to fight in World War 2, the Soldier was rejected from every branch of the U.S. military. Undaunted, he bought his own ticket to Europe. After arriving and finally locating Poland, the Soldier taught himself how to load and fire a variety of weapons before embarking on a Nazi killing spree for which he was awarded several medals that he designed and made himself. His rampage ended immediately upon hearing about the end of the war in 1949.

Pyro

Close-range fire-based class with airblast deflection.

The Pyro is a mysterious fire-wielding class that dominates in close-quarters combat with their Flamethrower. Either way, he's a fearsome, inscrutable, on-fire Frankenstein of a man - if he even is a man.

Heavy

Real Name Mikhail
Origin Russia

Tank class with massive health pool and sustained firepower.

The Heavy is the most durable class in the game, boasting a massive health pool and the ability to deal consistent, high damage with his Minigun. While his movement speed is reduced, his firepower is unmatched in sustained combat. Heavy is excellent for holding positions, protecting teammates, and suppressing enemy advances. His presence alone can dictate the flow of a battle.

Biography

Like a hibernating bear, the Heavy appears to be a gentle giant. Also like a bear, confusing his deliberate, sleepy demeanor with gentleness will get you ripped limb from limb. Though he speaks simply and moves with an economy of energy that's often confused with napping, the Heavy isn't dumb, he's not your big friend, and he generally wishes you'd just shut up before he has to make you shut up.

Medic

Real Name Dr. Herbert Ludwig
Origin Stuttgart, Germany

Support class with healing and powerful ÜberCharge abilities.

The Medic is essential to team success, providing constant healing and bonus HP to teammates through his Medigun. Medic's ability to heal and support multiple fronts on the battlefield makes him indispensable to any team looking to maintain control and sustain momentum. His signature ability, ÜberCharge, grants buffs like temporary invulnerability to himself and his patient, allowing aggressive plays and turning team fights. Building and managing ÜberCharge is critical to controlling the pace of battle.

Biography

What he lacks in compassion for the sick, respect for human dignity, and any sort of verifiable formal training in medicine, the Medic more than makes up for with a bottomless supply of giant needles and a trembling enthusiasm for plunging them into exposed flesh. Raised in Stuttgart, Germany during an era when the Hippocratic oath had been downgraded to an optional Hippocratic suggestion, the Medic considers healing a generally unintended side effect of satisfying his own morbid curiosity.
